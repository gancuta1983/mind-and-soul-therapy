Individual psychotherapy

A confidential space to pause, focus on yourself and explore what you are experiencing without judgement.

This may be helpful when you are experiencing:

  • Anxiety, panic attacks, depression, stress or insomnia
  • Low self-esteem or feelings of helplessness
  • Bereavement, loss or major life changes
  • Relationship difficulties or emotional overwhelm
  • A desire to understand yourself more deeply
Specialist emotional support

Motherhood

Motherhood can be deeply meaningful, but it can also feel overwhelming, isolating and emotionally complex.

  • Emotional overwhelm, anxiety or low mood
  • Changes in identity and confidence
  • Feeling isolated or unsupported
  • Relationship pressures and adjustment to a new role
  • Finding space for your own emotional needs
Before and after birth

Prenatal / Postnatal support

Support during pregnancy and after birth, with space to explore fears, losses, emotional changes and the transition into parenthood.

  • Anxiety or depression during pregnancy
  • Fear of birth or a difficult pregnancy
  • Pregnancy loss, birth trauma or postnatal depression
  • Feeling exhausted, disconnected or emotionally stretched
  • Online mother-and-baby sessions when childcare is difficult
