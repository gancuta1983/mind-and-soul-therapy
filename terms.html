Website terms and cancellations

Important information about using this website and arranging therapy.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Website information

The information on this website is general and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency support. Viewing the website or sending an enquiry does not create a therapist–client relationship.

Appointments

An appointment is confirmed only when agreed directly with Amalia and any required advance payment has been received. Therapy arrangements, confidentiality and professional boundaries will be set out in a therapy agreement.

Fees and cancellations

Current fees are shown on the fees page. Payment is made in advance by bank transfer. The notice period for cancellation or rescheduling, and any fee for late cancellation or missed appointments, will be confirmed in the therapy agreement before paid work begins.

Emergency support

Mind & Soul Therapy is not a crisis or emergency service. If you or another person is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. For urgent mental health help that is not an emergency, call NHS 111 and select the mental health option. Samaritans can be contacted free on 116 123.

External links

External links are provided for convenience. Mind & Soul Therapy is not responsible for the content, availability or privacy practices of third-party websites.
