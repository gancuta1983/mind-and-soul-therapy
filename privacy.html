Privacy policy

How Mind & Soul Therapy handles personal information submitted through this website.

Last updated: 26 July 2026

Information you provide

When you contact Mind & Soul Therapy, you may provide your name, contact details and a brief description of the support you are seeking. Please do not send detailed clinical or highly sensitive information through the website form.

How information is used

Your information is used to respond to your enquiry, arrange appointments and provide therapy services where agreed. It is not sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Contact form

The website form does not store your information on the website. It opens your email application and prepares a message addressed to Mind & Soul Therapy. The privacy and security of that message are then governed by your email provider and the recipient’s email provider.

Professional records

If you become a client, information relating to therapy will be handled under a separate therapy agreement and professional record-keeping arrangements explained before therapy begins.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information is held about you and request correction or deletion where applicable, subject to professional, safeguarding, insurance and legal record-retention duties.

Contact

For privacy questions, email amalia.counselling@gmail.com.
